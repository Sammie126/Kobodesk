1. Who we are

[YOUR COMPANY NAME] (RC [RC NUMBER]) decides how your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller. Contact us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. Our data protection officer is [NAME AND CONTACT].

2. What we collect

Your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash). Your bank account number, bank and account name. Your trade records, including amounts, wallet deposit addresses and times. If you verify your identity: your full name, date of birth, home address, and your BVN or NIN. Technical data such as your IP address and device, which we use for security. If you turn on two-factor sign-in, your authenticator key, which we store encrypted.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

To run your account and complete your trades (to perform our contract with you). To verify your identity, prevent fraud and meet anti-money-laundering rules (to meet legal obligations). To keep the service secure and fix problems (our legitimate interest). To send you messages about your trades and account (to perform our contract). We do not use your data to send advertising [CONFIRM WITH YOUR LAWYER].

4. Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run Kobodesk: our payment provider that sends naira to your bank, our crypto custody provider, our identity verification provider, and our email provider. [LIST BY NAME]. We share data with regulators and law enforcement when the law requires it. We do not sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and trade records for [NUMBER] years after your account closes, because financial and anti-money-laundering rules require it. After that we delete or anonymise them.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you may ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to delete it where we do not have to keep it, object to some uses, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Write to [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Identity numbers and authenticator keys are encrypted. Connections use HTTPS. Staff access to identity details is limited and recorded.

8. Cookies and local storage

We store a sign-in token in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising cookies. [IF YOU TURN ON ANALYTICS, DESCRIBE IT HERE].

9. Moving data abroad

Some of our providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we require them to protect it to a standard the law accepts. [CONFIRM WITH YOUR LAWYER].

10. Children

Kobodesk is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We will post any update here and tell you by email if the change is important.
