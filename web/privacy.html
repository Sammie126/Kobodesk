1. Who we are

[YOUR COMPANY NAME] (RC [RC NUMBER]) decides how your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller. Contact us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. Our data protection officer is [NAME AND CONTACT].

2. What we collect

Your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash). Your bank account number, bank and account name. Your trade records, including amounts, wallet deposit addresses and times. Before your first trade: your full name, date of birth, home address, your BVN or NIN, and a photo or PDF of your government-issued ID. Technical data such as your IP address and device, which we use for security. If you turn on two-factor sign-in, your authenticator key. A record of actions on your account and trades (an audit log), and a one-way fingerprint of your bank account number that lets us spot the same account on more than one customer account.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

To run your account and complete your trades (to perform our contract with you). To verify your identity, prevent fraud and meet anti-money-laundering rules (to meet legal obligations). To keep the service secure and fix problems (our legitimate interest). To send you messages about your trades and account (to perform our contract). We use simple automated rules, such as the number of trades in a short time, to pause an account and flag it for a person to review. A person makes the decision about holding, releasing or rejecting a trade. We do not use your data to send advertising [CONFIRM WITH YOUR LAWYER].

4. Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run Kobodesk: our payment provider that sends naira to your bank, our crypto custody provider, our identity verification provider (if we use one), our email provider, and our bot-protection provider (Cloudflare Turnstile), which receives technical signals from your browser when you sign up, log in or start a trade. [LIST EACH PROVIDER BY NAME]. We share data with regulators and law enforcement when the law requires it. We do not sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as we need it, and for as long as financial and anti-money-laundering rules require. [YOUR LAWYER MUST SET EACH PERIOD BELOW.]

Identity details and your ID file: [PERIOD] after your account closes or your last trade, whichever is later. Trade, payout and bank records: [PERIOD]. Audit logs: [PERIOD]. Security logs such as IP addresses: [PERIOD]. When a period ends, we delete or anonymise the data.

6. Your rights, and how to ask us to delete your data

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you may ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to some uses, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To ask us to delete your data: sign in, open Settings, and choose "Request deletion of my data". Or write to [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account. We will confirm your request and reply within [NUMBER] days. We will delete what we are allowed to delete. We may have to keep some records, such as identity and trade records, for the period in section 5, and we will tell you what we kept and why.

You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Your name, date of birth, address, BVN or NIN, ID file, bank account number and name, and authenticator key are encrypted before we store them. Connections use HTTPS. Staff access to identity details and ID files is limited and recorded in an audit log, and our admin pages require two-factor sign-in.

We store a sign-in token in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising cookies. [IF YOU TURN ON ANALYTICS, DESCRIBE IT HERE].

Some of our providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we require them to protect it to a standard the law accepts. [CONFIRM WITH YOUR LAWYER].

10. Children

Kobodesk is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We will post any update here and tell you by email if the change is important.
