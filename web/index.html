How it works

Three steps from wallet to bank

Step 1

Account Setup

Create your account once. It takes a few minutes, and you only do it the first time.

  • Sign up with your email. We send a 6-digit code to confirm it is really yours. The code works for 15 minutes.
  • Add your bank account. Choose your Nigerian bank and enter your account number. The account name comes back from the bank lookup so you can check it before you save.
  • Add extra protection if you like. Turn on two-factor sign-in, and verify your identity later to raise your trade limit.
Step 2

Choose Crypto & Get Address

See exactly what you will receive before you send anything.

  • Pick your coin and network. USDT, BTC, ETH or SOL, on the network your wallet will use.
  • Check your naira amount. We show it up front and lock the rate for a short time. A countdown on the trade page shows how long is left.
  • Copy your deposit address. It belongs to this trade only. The page shows the coin and network to use, so you can match them in your wallet.
Step 3

Send Crypto & Receive Naira

Send from your wallet, then follow each stage on your trade page. You can close the page and come back.

  • Network confirmations. Your transfer must be confirmed on the blockchain first. The number of confirmations needed depends on the coin and network.
  • Transaction verification. We match the deposit to your trade by address, coin and network. If the amount you send differs from the amount you entered, we pay for the amount we actually receive.
  • Bank payout. Once the deposit is verified, we send your naira to the bank account you saved and email you. Your receipt is ready when the trade shows Paid. Payout time: .

Security & trust

Your Security Comes First

These are the protections built into Kobodesk today, so you can see how your account, your details and your trades are looked after.

Account Protection

Only you should be able to get into your account.

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as readable text.
  • New accounts confirm their email with a one-time 6-digit code that expires after 15 minutes and allows only a few attempts.
  • Optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app adds a second check at login.
  • Repeated login attempts are slowed down, and you can log out of every device in one tap. Changing or resetting your password also signs out your other devices.

Data Protection

We keep sensitive details locked down and limit who sees them.

  • Your BVN or NIN and your two-factor key are encrypted before they are stored.
  • In our team's review lists, an ID number shows only its last 4 digits. Each time a team member opens the full details, the access is recorded.
  • The site sends browser security headers, and uses HTTPS with strict transport security when it is served over HTTPS.
  • Read what we collect and why in the Privacy Policy.

Transaction Verification

A trade is only paid out after the deposit has been checked.

  • Every trade gets its own deposit address, so a deposit can be matched to the right trade.
  • Deposit notices from our crypto provider are signature-checked before we act on them.
  • We wait for the required network confirmations before any naira is sent.
  • Each payout carries a reference unique to its trade, which is how a retry is matched to the original instead of being treated as a new payment.

Transparent Transactions

You can see what is happening to every trade.

  • The naira amount is shown before you start, and the rate is locked for a short time with a visible countdown.
  • Your trade page shows each stage: waiting for deposit, confirming, paying out, and paid.
  • You get an email when we see your deposit and when you are paid. A receipt shows the rate, fee and bank reference.
  • If a bank transfer fails, the trade is marked as needing attention and our team is alerted. Quote your trade ID to support.

Help us protect you. Use a password you do not use anywhere else, turn on two-factor sign-in, and never share your codes with anyone.
