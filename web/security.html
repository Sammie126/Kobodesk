Report a security issue

If you find a weakness in Kobodesk, we want to hear about it so we can fix it before anyone is harmed. Thank you for reporting it responsibly.

Where to send your report

Email . This address is also listed in our security.txt file.

What to include

What you found, the steps to reproduce it, the page or API address involved, and what an attacker could do with it. Screenshots or a short video help. Please write in English.

Please

Use only your own accounts and test data. Stop as soon as you have shown the problem, and do not read, change or keep other people's data. Do not run tests that slow down or break the service, send spam, or trick our staff or customers. Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you tell anyone else.

In scope

This website and its API at the address you are visiting.

Out of scope

Attacks that need physical access to a device, social engineering, denial-of-service testing, and weaknesses in the services of our providers. Report those to the provider directly.

What happens next

We read every report and write back to the address you used. We do not promise payment for reports.

Not a security issue? For help with a trade or your account, use the support details on the home page.
